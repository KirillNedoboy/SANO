# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful participation from people of every background and level
of experience. Keep discussion constructive, address ideas rather than people,
and accept feedback in good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks,
discrimination, and repeatedly disrupting discussion after a moderator asks
you to stop. Do not publish another person's private information.

Report a conduct concern privately through the maintainer contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include patient, family, genetic, or other
sensitive health information in the report. Maintainers may remove content,
restrict participation, or block an account in response to a violation.
